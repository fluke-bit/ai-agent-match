Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

AI Agent Match (“we”, “us”, the “site”) is a free directory that helps you find AI agents. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used. We keep it to a minimum: there are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

Information we collect

  • What you search for. The search box works inside your own browser, on a list of agents that loads with the page. What you type is not sent to us.
  • Server and security information. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), and its domain name is managed through Cloudflare. Like any website host, these providers process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of each request in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. They keep this information under their own policies.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it to reply.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not currently use analytics tools, show ads, or use advertising cookies.
  • If we add analytics, ads, accounts, or similar features in the future, we will update this policy before we do.

Cookies and browser storage

The site does not currently set cookies or store information in your browser.

Links to other sites

Each listing links to the agent’s page on another website, such as a marketplace run by its creator. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them. Agents you add may be able to read or act on your accounts and data. What they do with that information is governed by the agent’s creator and the platform that runs it, not by us.

How long we keep information

We keep emails only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. Our hosting providers keep technical logs under their own retention practices.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold almost no personal information, there is little to access or delete, but you are welcome to ask. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK, or California), you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@aiagentmatch.app
